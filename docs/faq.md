# TampifyAI — Frequently Asked Questions

## What does TampifyAI do?
TampifyAI is a Florida-based AI automation agency. We help local businesses get more customers and keep them coming back automatically, using AI agents, automation, and a modern web presence.

## How much does a website cost?
A standard business website starts at $500. It includes design, hosting setup, and a contact form. Sites needing booking systems or online stores are quoted separately.

## How do the AI agents work?
Our AI agents answer inquiries, qualify leads, follow up with customers, and book appointments. They run around the clock so you never miss a lead because you were busy or asleep.

## Do I need to be technical to use this?
No. We set everything up and hand it over ready to use. If you can send a text message, you can use it.

## Which businesses benefit most?
Gyms, restaurants, HVAC, roofing, med spas, dental and medical offices, real estate, cleaning and pest control, pet grooming, insurance, moving companies, and jewellers all benefit. Anywhere customers need a fast reply and a reason to come back.

## What happens after I book a call?
We review your current setup, identify where you are losing customers, and show you a 60-second demo of the AI agents working on your own type of business. If it is a fit, we set everything up for you.

## Is my customer data safe?
Yes. Data stays within your tools and accounts. We do not sell or share your customer information.

## Do you offer support?
Yes. Hosting and maintenance includes ongoing updates, backups, and small changes for a monthly fee, plus support when something needs attention.

## How do I get started?
Book a call through the website, or reply to any of our outreach emails and ask us to send the demo. If you reply with the word "send it", we will send you the 60-second demo video.
